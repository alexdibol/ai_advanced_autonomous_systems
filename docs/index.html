The architectural premise

The system itself
becomes a
research question.

A model produces an answer. An agent chooses an action. An advanced autonomous system can also participate in changing the procedures, relationships, and institutions through which its decisions are made.

The teaching is accessible; the subject is not elementary. These laboratories examine adaptability and collective complexity while keeping the underlying mechanisms open to inspection.

The discipline that survives

More autonomy.
More precise
questions.

The problem helps shape the organization that solves it. Evidence must establish whether that organization actually helps.
What is changing?Distinguish a revised answer, a revised search procedure, a revised team, and a revised institution.
What remains fixed?Identify the designed substrate: identities, permissions, registries, budgets, and selection mechanisms.
What counts as improvement?Separate selection scores from held-out performance, and a working demonstration from a general result.
Who authorizes the change?Trace proposal, evaluation, acceptance, implementation, and the record of what followed.

Intellectual and academic direction

Alejandro Reynoso

This collection connects artificial intelligence, quantitative finance, multi-agent systems, and institutional design. Its aim is to make advanced autonomous architectures understandable without overlooking their methodological complexity.

From improving a search procedure to negotiating the boundary between artificial societies, the central concern is the same: how intelligence can organize, adapt, and remain governable.
